# NEXT-REANALYSIS-CANDIDATE.md

## Decision

`PARTIAL_REANALYSIS_CANDIDATE`, not `NATURAL_REANALYSIS_CANDIDATE`.

The best overall candidate is the Liu et al. Human–AI preprint because it directly includes baseline, AI exposure, assisted performance and unassisted performance/persistence across experiments. Before reanalysis, verify preregistration, raw data, code, task assignment, scoring, confidence measures and license. It lacks long-horizon retention and broad far transfer.

The best public data candidate for open reanalysis is OULAD: strong timestamps, prior assessments, VLE exposure, later outcomes, failures/withdrawal and CC BY 4.0. It lacks randomized external exposure and a genuinely new transfer task. The offloading dataset is the cleanest public negative control for immediate performance versus later memory, but its task is narrow.

No reanalysis is executed in this mission. The next gate is a human decision after provenance audit.
