# INSTRUMENT-ATLAS.md

| INSTRUMENT | CONSTRUCT | VALIDATION | RELIABILITY | DOMAIN | UNIT | LICENSE | PUBLIC DATA | LIMITATIONS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Delayed post-test / transfer task | transfer/retention | experimental learning literature [1] [2] | depende do teste | education/general | individual | paper-specific | sometimes OSF | task similarity and practice effects |
| Worked-example + problem-solving protocol | schema acquisition/transfer | controlled experiments | task-specific | education | individual | paper-specific | often supplements | support may not fade |
| TMS scale | transactive memory | published team studies | reported in instrument literature | teams | team/member | usually research-use | mixed | self-report and domain specificity |
| SMM concept mapping/Pathfinder | shared mental model | validated comparative methods | task-specific | teams | team | research-use | mixed | similarity metric and task dependence |
| SAGAT/TSAGAT | situation awareness | aviation/operations literature | context-specific | complex teams | individual/team | research-use | rare | probe interruption and domain dependence |
| Network metrics | diversity/brokerage/constraint | network science | mathematical, not construct validity | organizations/science | node/team | open methods | many public graphs | structural diversity ≠ information diversity |
| Cognitive offloading logs | externalization/memory | controlled experiments [6] | direct behavioral measures | lab | individual | paper-specific | OSF sometimes | often short-term and simple tasks |
| AI removal test | augmentation vs learning/dependence | emerging RCTs [7] | study-specific | math/reading/knowledge work | individual | preprint/paper-specific | not yet standardized | removal may measure persistence more than skill |

Nenhum instrumento validado cobre simultaneamente baseline, exposição, transferência, retenção, adaptação e independência.
