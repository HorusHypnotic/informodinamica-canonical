# HUMAN-AI-EVIDENCE.md

| Pattern | Evidence | Classification | What it does not show |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human + AI > human alone during task | medical decision experiment [5], collective/human–AI literature | PERFORMANCE_AUGMENTATION | residual learning or transfer |
| AI improves immediate output but later memory drops under offloading | offloading RCT [6] | AUGMENTATION + retention cost | generative AI specifically |
| AI assistance followed by worse unassisted performance/persistence | three experiments, N=1,222, preprint [7] | DEPENDENCE/negative independent outcome | definitive deskilling over long horizon |
| AI protocol measuring effort with eye tracking/fNIRS | RCT protocol [4] | DESIGN, not result | learning/retention until results exist |

The hard map is: A augmentation is common; B learning after removal is rarely measured; C transfer is rarer; D dependence has emerging evidence; E deskilling needs longer follow-up and baseline comparison; F false confidence requires calibrated confidence measures and was not established by the sources above.
