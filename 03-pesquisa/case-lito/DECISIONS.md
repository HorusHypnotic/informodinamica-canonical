# CASE-LITO — DECISIONS

## D-001 — Public evidence is not a clinical record
Accepted. Social/news material may generate leads and chronology candidates, but patient-specific clinical claims require appropriate authority.

## D-002 — Preserve failed/weakened hypotheses
Accepted. The visual-loss + preserved-cognition atypicality signal remains in history with status WEAKENED after Heidenhain counterevidence.

## D-003 — Access research is separate from eligibility
Accepted. Trial recruitment mapping may be performed; individual eligibility/enrollment may not be inferred.

## D-004 — No silent overwrite
Accepted. Updates to chronology, trial state or hypotheses should retain date/source/history so later knowledge does not erase prior state.
