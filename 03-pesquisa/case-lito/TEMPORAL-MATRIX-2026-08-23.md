## Interpretation guardrails
- Do not infer cognitive integrity from ability to appear in a video or communicate publicly.
- Do not infer symptom onset from first social-media disclosure.
- Do not infer negative test results from absence of reporting.
- Do not label Heidenhain or another molecular subtype from public chronology alone.

## Research use
Populate only when a source can carry the required authority. Each update should preserve prior values/history rather than overwrite silently.
