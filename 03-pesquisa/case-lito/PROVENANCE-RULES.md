# CASE-LITO — PROVENANCE RULES

For each patient-specific observation preserve: source, publication/observation date when known, source authority, whether direct or second-hand, semantic resolution, uncertainty, and revision history.

For trial claims preserve: registry/sponsor/site identity, checked-at date and exact state. Conflicting layers remain separate instead of being reconciled by assumption.

For literature claims preserve population, design and scope; do not transfer cohort-level findings directly to an individual.
