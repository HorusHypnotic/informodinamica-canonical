# CASE-LITO — ADVERSARIAL REVIEW 001

Date: 2026-08-23
Verdict: PRIOR SIGNAL WEAKENED

## Target claim
Candidate interpretation: visual loss together with relatively preserved lucidity/cognition may be a strong atypicality signal against the expected sCJD trajectory.

## Breaker evidence
Published Heidenhain-variant literature documents sCJD cases with isolated/early visual symptoms and cognitive decline delayed for weeks. A pathologically confirmed UK series found 22/594 sCJD cases with isolated visual symptoms at onset. Later cohorts also describe heterogeneous first non-visual manifestations, including cognitive decline and unsteady gait, and show that serial MRI/EEG can increase diagnostic yield.

## Result
The feature combination is not sufficiently discriminating by itself. The earlier signal is downgraded and preserved as part of the research history rather than deleted.

## What would discriminate better
- authoritative ordering of first neurological manifestations;
- dated MRI DWI/FLAIR findings, preferably serial;
- RT-QuIC and other CSF results;
- EEG timing/findings;
- PRNP/codon 129 and molecular subtype data where clinically obtained.

## Constraint
No conclusion about the patient's subtype or diagnostic correctness follows from this review.
