# Cover Letter – Journal of Systems and Software

**To:** Editor-in-Chief, Journal of Systems and Software  
**Date:** August 6, 2026  

**Subject:** Submission of original methodological paper: "Una Metodologia para Análise da Persistência da Coordenação em Sistemas Sociotécnicos"

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Uma Metodologia para Análise da Persistência da Coordenação em Sistemas Sociotécnicos" for consideration for publication in the *Journal of Systems and Software*. 

This paper introduces a formal methodology—comprising protocols MET-006 through MET-009, informodynamic latencies ($T_0$–$T_4$), and a three-tier entropy taxonomy—designed to analyze, diagnose, engineer, and continuously calibrate coordination persistence in sociotechnical systems. To demonstrate its operational utility, we document a reproducible reference laboratory implemented within a Continuous Integration / Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipeline (EXP-001), evaluating eight experimental conditions and addressing the critical phenomenon of "false greens."

We believe this work fits exceptionally well within the scope of JSS, as it bridges theoretical foundations in software engineering and sociotechnical systems with empirical, reproducible experimentation. All data, code, and modular components are openly versioned in our canonical repository.

Thank you for your time and consideration. We look forward to your review.

Sincerely,
